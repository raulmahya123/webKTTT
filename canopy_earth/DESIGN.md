## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, editorial, and tactile visual identity dedicated to frontline environmental governance, community forestry, and indigenous rights advocacy. It reflects rigorous investigative credibility balanced with the warmth, organic grounding, and resilience of community stewardship across maritime and rainforest landscapes.

### Design Movement: Modern Editorial Stewardship
The style integrates contemporary editorial journalism with organic, tactile authenticity:
- **Editorial Rigor:** High-contrast typographic hierarchy, generous rhythmic whitespace, and disciplined vertical proportions reminiscent of premium environmental periodicals and investigative white papers.
- **Organic Tactility:** Natural, non-synthetic hues, subtle paper-textured cream backgrounds, and refined hairline rules that evoke field notebooks and botanical cartography rather than sterile enterprise SaaS interfaces.
- **Restraint & Substance:** Avoids trendy neon accents, arbitrary rounded pill containers, and synthetic glowing cards. Instead, interactive elements use deliberate borders, warm earth tones, and confident typography to signal enduring institutional trust.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the canopy, soil, and field notes of Southeast Asian conservation:

- **Primary (`#184D3B` - Deep Forest Green):** The commanding anchor color. Used for prominent headers, primary action buttons, key brand banners, and essential navigation states. It communicates longevity, vitality, and forest protection.
- **Secondary (`#A4B9A1` - Sage Green):** A balancing, muted foliage tone. Used for supporting tags, category pills, subtle iconography backgrounds, and progress indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#C28B59` - Earth Ochre):** Warm clay and loam. Applied strategically for actionable calls to action (e.g., investigative report downloads, campaign highlights, urgent alerts) to inject human warmth without the hostility of standard warning reds.
- **Neutral (`#202B25` - Charcoal Bark):** The principal ink color for body copy, high-priority captions, and structural borders. Provides deeper legibility and natural warmth compared to stark pitch-black.
- **Canvas (`#F8F7F2` - Fieldwork Cream) & (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** The primary canvas is warm unbleached paper cream, setting an archival editorial tone. Pure white is reserved for foreground cards, reports, and clean image backdrops to introduce depth.

Ensure contrast ratios exceed WCAG AAA standards for all primary editorial text against the cream and white surfaces.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the structured geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines with the clean neutrality of **Inter** for sustained reading:

- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Crafted with tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and firm font weights (600, 700, 800) to convey investigative clarity, authority, and editorial gravitas.
- **Body & Editorial Content (Inter):** Set with generous line heights (`1.6` to `1.7`) and standard body sizes (`16px` to `18px`) to ensure prolonged readability across investigative reports, legal briefs, and community field journals.
- **Metadata, Badges & Labels (Inter Semibold):** Scaled down with uppercase or capitalized styling and expanded letter spacing (`+0.04em` to `+0.06em`) to reflect archival tagging and scientific classification.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed around an editorial broadsheet grid:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** A 12-column grid with a maximum content container of `1280px`, `4.5rem` outer margins, and `2.5rem` gutters. Accommodates asymmetrical splits (such as 8-column investigative text paired with a 4-column field data sidebar).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** An 8-column layout with `2.5rem` side margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Multi-column cards collapse gracefully into pairs.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** A 4-column layout with `1.25rem` side margins and `1rem` gutters. Text stacks vertically to prioritize reading rhythm.

Rhythmic vertical cadence relies on multiples of `8px` (`0.5rem`). Long-form articles and narrative sections utilize generous vertical separation (`space-xl` to `4.5rem`) to ensure room for high-impact photojournalism.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally departs from synthetic software shadows, using physical paper layering, hairline boundaries, and deep atmospheric tones instead:

- **Zero-Shadow Default:** Baseline surfaces sit directly on the `#F8F7F2` canvas with fine `1px` borders colored in `#E3E0D5` (Sand Border) or `#A4B9A1` (Muted Sage).
- **Layered Paper Elevation:** Distinct content blocks (cards, report previews) use a solid `#FFFFFF` fill framed by `1px solid #E3E0D5`. Interactive hover states shift the border to `#184D3B` and introduce an ultra-subtle, warm directional contact shadow: `0 4px 12px rgba(32, 43, 37, 0.06)`.
- **Field Overlays & Mega Menus:** Navigation panels, overlays, and modal drawers emerge with crisp, opaque backgrounds framed in `#184D3B`, supported by a deep organic backdrop dim: `rgba(24, 77, 59, 0.40)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft (`1`)** geometry:

- **Standard Elements (0.25rem / 4px):** Applied to buttons, form inputs, callout containers, and image masks to maintain crisp structure reminiscent of archival documents and printed press.
- **Compound Cards (`rounded-lg` / 0.5rem / 8px):** Used on overarching report containers, map callouts, and data summary cards.
- **Category Chips & Badges (`9999px` / Full Pill):** Used exclusively on compact status badges and publication taxonomy chips to provide a clear interactive contrast against rectilinear content blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#184D3B` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `4px` border radius, `12px 24px` padding, font-weight `600`. Hover state transitions to `#0F3226` with a `1px` translation offset.
- **Secondary / Action (Campaign & Support):** Solid `#C28B59` background, `#FFFFFF` text. Focus state rings with `#C28B59` at `2px` offset.
- **Outline / Ghost:** `#FFFFFF` or transparent fill, `1.5px` border in `#184D3B`, text in `#184D3B`. Hover state applies `#184D3B` background and `#FFFFFF` text.

### Mega Menu & Navigation
- **Header:** Off-white `#F8F7F2` background with a slim bottom boundary (`1px solid #E3E0D5`). Includes brand mark, clean horizontal primary links (`15px`, `font-weight: 600`), language toggle (`ID / EN`), and a distinct action button.
- **Mega Menu Dropdown:** Solid `#FFFFFF` panel with an asymmetrical 3-column layout: Featured Editorial Report (left), Direct Thematic Links (center: Forests, Ocean, Indigenous Rights), and Real-time Incident Tracker / Ground Investigation links (right). Framed by `1px solid #E3E0D5`.

### Editorial Cards
- **Publication & Investigation Cards:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E3E0D5` perimeter border. Includes high-resolution photographic header with an archival ratio (`16:10`), followed by a taxonomy badge (`#A4B9A1` light tint), bold headline (`headline-sm`), reading time/date metadata, and an inline text link with directional arrow indicator.
- **Interactive States:** On hover, the image subtly scales (`1.02`), and the border color shifts smoothly to `#184D3B`.

### Tags & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Pill-shaped, compact padding (`4px 10px`), uppercase `label-sm`.
- **Forestry / Advocacy:** Sage background (`#E8EFE7`) with deep green text (`#184D3B`).
- **Urgent / Investigation:** Soft sand background (`#F5ECE3`) with ochre text (`#C28B59`).

### Form Elements & Inputs
- **Text Inputs:** `#FFFFFF` surface, `1px solid #E3E0D5` border, `4px` radius, `12px 16px` padding. Placeholder text in `#6E8771`. Active focus transitions border to `#184D3B` with zero outer blur ring.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `18px` square or circular box with `1.5px` `#184D3B` border. Filled with `#184D3B` and crisp white check icon when selected.

### Case Tracker & Map Data Callouts
- Compact, high-density data boxes with `#F8F7F2` background, hairline divider lines, monospaced coordinate tags, and clear status indicators representing field verifications and community alerts.
